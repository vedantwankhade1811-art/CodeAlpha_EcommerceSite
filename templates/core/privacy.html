DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Cartza Studio India Pvt. Ltd. is deeply committed to upholding your trust and safeguarding your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal data necessary to provide seamless e-commerce services, fulfill your orders, and enhance your digital experience:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Name, email address, contact telephone number, and account credentials when you register.
  • Fulfillment Information: Physical delivery address, billing address, PIN code, and recipient telephone numbers for courier dispatch.
  • Transaction Records: History of products purchased, order values, invoices, and payment confirmation IDs. We do not store complete credit card numbers or banking passwords — all transactions are processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser version, device category, IP address, and cookie tokens to preserve your shopping bag across browsing sessions.

2. How We Utilize Your Data

Your personal data is processed strictly for the following lawful objectives:

  • Fulfilling and tracking product shipments, exchanges, and warranty obligations.
  • Transmitting transactional alerts (order confirmation, invoice PDFs, dispatch tracking numbers).
  • Preventing unauthorized fraudulent transactions and maintaining account integrity.
  • Providing responsive concierge customer service when requested by you.

3. Zero-Sale Policy & Third-Party Disclosure

We never sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal information to third-party data brokers or external advertisers under any circumstances.

Information is shared only with strictly vetted service providers bound by non-disclosure agreements:

  • Logistics Couriers: Delivery address and phone numbers are shared solely with our courier partners (e.g., Blue Dart, Delhivery) for final-mile doorstep delivery.
  • Payment Processors: Secure PCI-compliant gateways for instantaneous payment settlement.
  • Statutory Compliance: When required by binding legal process or law enforcement under Indian jurisdiction.

4. Data Retention & Security Controls

All communications between your browser and Cartza servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 with 256-bit encryption). Data at rest is isolated and encrypted using AES-256 standards in ISO-certified Indian data centers.

5. Your Statutory Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) framework and applicable laws, you retain the full right to:

  • Access a copy of all personal information maintained in your Cartza profile.
  • Request correction or rectification of outdated contact and address information.
  • Request complete deletion or erasure of your customer profile and account credentials.
  • Opt-out of non-transactional marketing and newsletter emails at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@cartza.com.
