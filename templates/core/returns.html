7-DAY RESTFUL GUARANTEE

Returns & Exchange Policy

If any piece fails to delight you in fit, tactile feel, or finish, we provide complimentary doorstep return and exchange with no questions asked.

How to Initiate a Return or Exchange

Step 1

Submit Request

Visit your Account Profile or email concierge@cartza.com within 7 calendar days of receipt.

Step 2

Doorstep Collection

Our courier partner will arrive at your address within 24–48 hours to collect the packaged parcel. No printing of shipping labels required.

Step 3

Refund or Replacement

Upon arrival at our inspection studio, your replacement is dispatched immediately or your full refund is credited back to your original payment method in 3–5 working days.

Condition & Eligibility Guidelines

To qualify for a restful return or exchange, items must satisfy the following honest criteria:

✓
Original Unaltered State

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and free of perfumes, makeup marks, or pet hair, with all original Cartza tags and spare buttons intact.

✓
Footwear & Leather Goods

Shoes must only be tried on clean, carpeted surfaces. Footwear returned with scuffed soles or creased vamps will be subject to rejection.

✓
Hygienic Essentials

Personal grooming items, face masks, or items explicitly labelled “Final Archive Sale” are not eligible for exchange or return due to sanitary considerations.
